How to set up your signature

  1. In the Your details section below, type your name into the Name field.
  2. Type your job title into the Title field.
  3. Type your region into the Region field.
  4. Type your work email into the Email field.
  5. Type your phone number into the Phone field.
  6. Untick Region, Email or Phone if you do not want that line to appear. Title, website and social icons always show.
  7. If you are promoting a show, tick Include a promo banner and fill in the show details.
  8. Look at the Preview and check everything is correct.
  9. Select the Copy signature button.
  10. Open Outlook.
  11. Go to your signature settings.
    1. Classic Outlook: select File, then Options, then Mail, then Signatures.
    2. New Outlook or Outlook on the web: select Settings, then Mail, then Compose and reply.
  12. Select New to create a signature, and give it a name.
  13. Click inside the large editing box.
  14. Paste with Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac.
  15. Select Save.
  16. Send yourself a test email to check it looks right.

Do not retype or edit anything once it is in Outlook. Outlook rewrites the layout when you edit inside it, which resizes the logo and breaks the spacing. If something needs changing, change it on this page and paste again.
