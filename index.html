Sawgrass Signature Builder

Fill it in, copy it, paste into Outlook once. Nothing gets edited inside Outlook.

How to use it

  1. Fill in your details and untick anything you don't want.
  2. Click Copy signature.
  3. Outlook: File → Options → Mail → Signatures. New Outlook or web: Settings → Mail → Compose and reply.
  4. Create a new signature, paste, save. Don't retype anything in there. Editing inside Outlook is what resizes the logo and breaks the spacing.
